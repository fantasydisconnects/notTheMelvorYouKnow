# Changes
- Completely reworked Sigils
  - Reasons:
    - Firstly, they were shamelessly almost 1-for-1 copied from Guild Wars 2, and I have since acquired the ability to come up with original ideas (if still mostly based on that game's Sigils and Runes)
    - Secondly, they felt too chaotic, confusing, unwieldy to craft, and just not in the spirit of the game
  - The Details:
    - Firstly, Sigils have been removed as Equipment items and have instead been made into Enhancements. As of this updates, only Lesser Sigils exist. Test them out!
    - Secondly, crafting Sigils has been made much more streamlined. No longer do you have to search for several unique components just to create a specific Sigil. Each Sigil now follows this basic process:
      - Acquire Marksman's, Knight's, and Wizard's Sigils from Thieving or Combat
      - Extract Nature, Body, and Mind Essence from those Sigils by casting their respective spells in Alt. Magic
      - Create a Sigil in the Special Runes & Sigils Category in Runecrafting using a Silver/Gold/Platinum Bar, 1 Nature/Body/Mind Essence, 10 Golden Stardust/Golden Star/Golden Star II, and a combination of Runes
- Added 1-5 Marksman's Sigils as a possible rare drop from Bandit Chest, and as a very rare drop from Thief
- Added 1-5 Knight's Sigils as a possible rare drop from Raid Loot, and as a very rare drop from Paladin
- Added 1-5 Wizard's Sigils as a possible rare drop from Magic Chest, and as a very rare drop from Elementalist
- Removed unused tertiary ingredients from the game:
  - Hoof, Fang, Large Fang, Massive Fang, Celestial Feather, Phoenix Feather
- Removed gem dust and enchanted gem dust from the game
- Moved Elemental Gear Upgrades to Runecrafting in the Air/Water/Earth/Fire Gear categories. Their recipes no longer require Ancient Gear, and instead now require:
  - 84 Runecrafting for all Air Elementalist Gear, Nature Runes, Air Runes, and Eyes of Air
  - 88 Runecrafting for all Water Elementalist Gear, Nature Runes, Water Runes, and Eyes of Water
  - 92 Runecrafting for all Earth Elementalist Gear, Nature Runes, Earth Runes, and Eyes of Earth
  - 96 Runecrafting for all Fire Elementalist Gear, Nature Runes, Fire Runes, and Eyes of Fire
- Moved Elemental Staves Upgrades to Runecrafting in the Staves & Wands category. Their recipes no longer require Ancient Staves, and instead now require:
  - 84 Runecrafting, Redwood Logs, Nature Runes, Air Runes, Eyes of Air, and Mystic Air Staves
  - 88 Runecrafting, Redwood Logs, Nature Runes, Water Runes, Eyes of Water, and Mystic Air Staves
  - 92 Runecrafting, Redwood Logs, Nature Runes, Earth Runes, Eyes of Earth, and Mystic Air Staves
  - 96 Runecrafting, Redwood Logs, Nature Runes, Fire Runes, Eyes of Fire, and Mystic Air Staves

SAE v0.9.4.8 Patch Notes
# Content
- Added Draconis Rex, a new Master-tier, randomized attack style boss and potential Slayer Task found in Challenges which grants a unique pet after 300 kills, and has a 1/128 and 1/256 chance to drop two new items, Rex Heads and Blood of the King. It also is able to drop the eponymous Dragonfire shield, at a very rare rate of 1/1,000.
  - Balance wise, Draconis Rex falls somewhere between Malcs, Guardian of Melvor and Glacia. It uses special attacks from both, as well as new dragonbreath variants that are slightly more powerful than the Perilous Peaks dragons'. If you can clear the Water God Dungeon, you should find Draconis Rex to be doable.
  - Rex Heads can be used to upgrade the Slayer's Crossbow into the Dragonslayer Crossbow, a new best-in-slot ranged weapon against Draconic creatures. It can also be cooked with level 90 Cooking to create 3 Fried Dragon Heads, which grants 350 Cooking XP.
